COMMUNITY REMINDERS

A Few Reminders for Our Homeowners

Small reminders that help keep our community clean, safe, peaceful, and accessible for everyone.

🧹

Cleanliness

1. Waste Disposal Schedule.

♻️ Garbage collection is scheduled every Wednesday and Saturday. Please place your trash in proper garbage bags and put them in front of your house for collection. Improperly disposed garbage may not be collected and may be subject to HOA reprimand.

2. Keep sidewalks, streets & drainage clear.

🚯 Huwag magtapon, mag-ipon ng basura, o mag-discharge ng maruming tubig sa kalsada at drainage.

🏠

Home & Property

1. Keep your property clean and sanitary.

🏡 Panatilihing malinis ang harapan at paligid ng bahay. Avoid conditions that attract pests or create health hazards.

2. Check before building, extending, or modifying.

🔨 Bago mag-extend, magtayo ng structure, o gumawa ng alteration, siguraduhing compliant sa permits, zoning, building rules, at HOA guidelines.

🔊

Noise

1. Be mindful of your neighbors, especially at night.

🔇 Videoke, speakers, power tools, at iba pang maingay na activities should be controlled.

10:00 PMNoise-producing equipment reminder

2. Avoid excessive or disturbing noise.

🤫 Hindi porke nasa loob ng sariling bahay, okay na ang anumang level ng ingay. Be considerate of your neighbors.

🚗

Parking

1. Don't block roads, sidewalks, driveways, or access.

🚫 Public roads are for everyone—huwag gawing permanent parking space o harangan ang daanan.

2. Park within your property whenever possible.

🏠 Kung may sariling garage/carport, gamitin ito. Iwasang gawing extension ng bahay ang public road.

ⓘ

These reminders are based on applicable City of Bacoor ordinances and regulations. Homeowners are encouraged to observe both city requirements and HOA guidelines.
